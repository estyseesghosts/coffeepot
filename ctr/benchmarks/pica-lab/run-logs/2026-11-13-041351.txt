  - Self-test: all three runs pass.
  - Repeatability: 167 of 167 non-perf hashes are identical across the three

    these tests.
  - Run time: a full run takes about 36–46 s. About 25 s of that is writing 130
    raster and edge dumps to the SD card.
  - Probe: GSPGPU_ReadHWRegs works. The ID register reads 0x00010002 and the

  - Blend: it follows the expected blend math to within about 1 LSB. For
    example, at i=1 the red channel reads 0x48, which matches the arithmetic. At
    i=11 it reads 0x99 where I compute about 0x98.
  - TEV: output rises by exactly 8 per step on red. This sweep doesn't expose
    any rounding quirk.

  What the logs say about the raster test
  - No direction effect: forward and reverse sweeps are identical in all 64
    steps. There's no hysteresis or ordering artifact.
  - Coarse snap grid: across 64 steps of 1/64 px, only 6 distinct images appear,
    with transitions at steps 9, 25, 32, 41 and 57. Those sit about 16 steps
    (1/4 px) apart, plus one at the half-pixel step. So the effective x snap
    here looks like about 1/4 px, not 1/64. This is a hypothesis from one
    geometry. Nothing here confirms it.

  What the logs say about the harness
  - Edge test is invalid: both captures cover only about half the quad (8224 and
    8256 of 16384 pixels). reverse_diag lies entirely on one side of the
    diagonal. Both triangles are written into the same vertex slots before the
    GPU reads them, so the second triangle is drawn twice. This is a harness
    bug.
  - Perf is a null result: 1 draw and 64 draws both have a median of 0.414 ms,
    matching to about 0.001 ms in all three runs. That points to a fixed cost,
    most likely the target clear and framebuffer flush, dominating the
    measurement. It does not show that draw calls are free.
