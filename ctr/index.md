# Nintendo 3DS (CTR)

The Nintendo 3DS is a handheld console sold from 2011, the successor to the Nintendo DS. Its signature feature is a glasses-free stereoscopic 3D upper screen, paired with a touchscreen lower screen.

It plays 3DS cartridges and (mostly) DS cartridges, and runs a Nintendo operating system the company calls [Horizon](../glossary.md#horizon). The family includes the 3DS, 3DS XL, 2DS, and the faster New 3DS / New 3DS XL / New 2DS XL models. This wiki uses **CTR** ([glossary](glossary.md#ctr)) as the platform name for the whole family.
